Biography

About the artist

Jono Vengo is a contemporary artist whose practice blends painting, collage, and textured materials to create work rooted in atmosphere and memory. Their compositions draw from long walks, changing seasons, and the emotional imprint of familiar places.

Working from a light-filled studio, Jono builds each piece through layers of color and gesture, allowing fragments to emerge slowly over time. The result is a body of work that feels both intimate and expansive, balancing structure with spontaneity.

Over the past decade, Jono has exhibited in group and solo shows, collaborated with local creative communities, and produced commissions for collectors seeking artwork with warmth and a strong sense of story.
